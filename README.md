# Data Science Research Deep Dives

This repository is where I document my learning from **Data Science and Machine Learning research papers**.

I started this because I want to understand the ideas behind the models and techniques that I use in projects, instead of only using them as libraries or APIs.

My approach is:

**Read → Understand → Question → Explain → Document** 

I write the concepts in my own words and try to connect the research ideas with what I already know from Data Science and Machine Learning.

## 🧠 What I Focus On

For each paper, I try to understand:

* What problem is the paper solving?
* Why is the problem important?
* What is the main idea?
* How does the proposed method work?
* What mathematics is behind the method?
* What assumptions are being made?
* What experiments did the authors perform?
* What did I actually understand from the paper?
* What questions do I still have?
* How does the research connect to practical Machine Learning?
